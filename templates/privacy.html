Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

W2W Sports is a free service built by two college students. We keep things simple — we collect as little data as possible and never sell anything to anyone.

What We Collect

The only personal information we collect is your email address, and only if you choose to subscribe to our daily digest. We do not collect your name, location, or any other identifying information.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site — things like which pages are visited and how long people stay. This data is anonymous and aggregated. You can opt out via Google's standard opt-out tools.

How We Use Your Email

  • Daily digest only. We send one email per day with the top sports matchups. Nothing else.
  • No third-party sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share your email with anyone.
  • Easy unsubscribe. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email us directly to be removed.

Email Service

We use Resend to send emails. Your email address is stored in our database and shared with Resend solely for the purpose of delivering the digest. Resend's privacy policy is available at resend.com.

Data Storage

Email addresses are stored securely in a Supabase database hosted in the United States. We retain your email only for as long as you remain subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your email is deleted from our database.

Your Rights

You can request to have your data deleted at any time by unsubscribing or emailing us directly. If you're in the EU or California, you have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively — we'll honor any request to access, correct, or delete your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at giffor@bc.edu or gansonv@bc.edu.
