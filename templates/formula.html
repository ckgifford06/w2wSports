The W2W Score Formula

Page is still in progress...

The W2W Score is a proprietary ranking system that evaluates every major U.S. sporting event across the NBA, NFL, NHL, and MLB to determine which matchups are the most exciting to watch on any given day.

Core Components

We take into account...

  • Rivalries
  • Marketability
  • Competitiveness
  • Quality of Play
  • Importance

We think that these five factors are what determines the watchability (W2W Score) of a game.

How It’s Calculated

First, we assign a specific 1-10 marketability value to each team. Then, we assign another value to each rivalry in each sport, valuing them on a scale of 1-10, just like the marketability value. After that, we take each teams record, calculate the win differential, subtract it from 20, and divide by 5. This gives us our competitiveness value. Our competitiveness value finds how close these teams are in the standings. Next, we calculate the quality of play. This is determined by how well the teams are playing. We do this by getting their combined win percentage, multiplying by 10, and dividing by 3. Finally, we calculate the importance of the game. Obviously, the first game of the season is not nearly as important as game 7, so we calculate this by retrieving the amount of games left and assigning a value to each interval of 20.

This is how we calculate our W2W Score.

Final Formula

Rivalry + Marketability + Competitiveness + Quality of Play + Importance of Game = W2W Score
