The W2W Score Formula

The W2W Score is a proprietary ranking system that evaluates every major U.S. sporting event across the NBA, NFL, NHL, and MLB to determine which matchups are the most exciting to watch on any given day.

Our algorithm blends statistics, storylines, and fan energy into one daily rating. Each category contributes a weighted percentage to create a total score out of 100. Here’s how it breaks down:

Core Components

  • Rivalry Index (25%) — Detects historical rivalries, divisional tension, and recent rematches.
  • Competitiveness (25%) — Evaluates team records, point differentials, and expected game closeness.
  • Marketability (20%) — Measures player star power, fan following, and national TV appeal.
  • Quality of Play (15%) — Uses recent performance metrics, offensive/defensive efficiency, and streaks.
  • Game Importance (15%) — Accounts for playoff impact, standings implications, and late-season stakes.

How It’s Calculated

Each factor is normalized on a 0–10 scale using live data from ESPN APIs and custom Python modules (NBArating, NFLrating, NHLrating, MLBrating). The formula looks like this:

W2W Score = (0.25 × Rivalry) + (0.25 × Competitiveness) + (0.20 × Marketability) + (0.15 × Quality) + (0.15 × Importance)

Scores are then sorted daily to generate our Top 10 Matchups leaderboard — giving fans a quick, data-driven way to decide which games are truly worth watching.

Why It Matters

We believe sports are about stories and energy, not just stats. The W2W Score is designed to highlight the games that feel the biggest — where the numbers and the narratives meet. Whether it’s Celtics–Lakers, Yankees–Red Sox, or a surprise playoff chase, our formula finds the matchups that define the day in sports.
