About W2W Sports

W2W Sports scores every game across the NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, CFB, CBB, and EPL each day and ranks them by how worth watching they actually are.

How It Works

Each game gets a W2W Score based on five components:

  • Rivalry Historical and divisional matchups score higher.
  • Marketability Based on each team's national following and star power.
  • Competitiveness Teams with similar records tend to produce closer games.
  • Quality of Play Win percentage across the season.
  • Importance Playoff races, seeding battles, and postseason games score higher.

See Our Formula for the full methodology.

Background

Built in October 2025 by two BC students who wanted a single number to answer the nightly question of what to watch. All game data comes from the ESPN API and updates daily.
