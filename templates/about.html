About W2W Sports

W2W Sports scores every game across the NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, CFB, CBB, EPL, UCL, WNBA, and MMA each day and ranks them by how worth watching they actually are.

Background

W2W was built in October 2025 by two Boston College students who wanted a single (and understandable) number to answer the nightly question of what to watch. Living with each other as two fans of rivalrying cities (New York and Boston), we would constantly bicker about what games to watch. Since then, we have had no doubt.
