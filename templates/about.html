About W2W Sports

W2W Sports rates and ranks the most watchable games each day across the NBA, WNBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, CFB, CBB, EPL, UCL, and MMA.

Every day we score every scheduled game and give it a single W2W Score. The higher the score, the more it's worth watching. See Our Formula for the full breakdown.

Our Mission

W2W exists to help you find the best games to watch — a heated rivalry, a playoff race, a rising underdog — without scrolling through every schedule to figure out what's actually on.

How It Works

Each matchup is scored on five factors:

  • Rivalry Divisional and historical battles get a boost.
  • Marketability Star power and popularity raise the rating.
  • Competitiveness Close records mean tighter games and a better watch.
  • Quality of Play Team performance and consistency over the season.
  • Importance Late-season and playoff-impacting games rank higher.

How We Started

We started W2W Sports in October 2025 because we were tired of scrolling through game schedules trying to figure out which matchups were actually worth watching. As lifelong sports fans — New York and Boston — we wanted a way to turn stats, storylines, and hype into one clear score. We hope you enjoy the site.
