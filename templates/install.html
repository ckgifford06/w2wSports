Add to Home Screen

Install W2W Sports on your iPhone in three taps. It opens like a real app with no browser bar.

1
Open in Safari
Make sure you're using Safari. This won't work from Chrome or another browser.
2
Tap the Share button
At the bottom of your screen, tap the box with an arrow pointing up.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share sheet until you see "Add to Home Screen" and tap it. Then tap Add in the top right.

W2W Sports will appear on your home screen with its own icon. Tap it to open the app without any browser chrome.
